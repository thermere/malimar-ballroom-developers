# Vision

## Purpose

Malimar Ballroom explores how a conversation with several AI voices can help a person think a question through. Each voice is a fictional specialist with a fixed role. The specialists answer together in one room, so a person sees where they agree, where they disagree, and what evidence each one relies on.

## Principles

- **Fictional by design.** Every specialist is fictional. No seat may imitate a real person, an office holder, a court, a firm or a company.
- **General information only.** The room is not a source of legal, tax, investment or medical advice.
- **Evidence over confidence.** A specialist must not invent statutes, cases, quotes, rates or figures. A challenge prompts a check of the evidence; it does not prove a claim.
- **Say what is not known.** When evidence is missing or conflicts, the answer says so.
- **Private by default.** Records stay in the visitor's browser unless the visitor chooses to save or share them. The app works without an account.
- **Usable by everyone.** The app is meant to work at phone width, in light and dark themes, and with reduced motion.
- **Honest about limits.** Passing automated tests shows that code behaves as written on the cases tested. It does not show live-service quality, real-device behaviour or accuracy.

## What it is not

- It is not a finished product. Most parts are prototypes or planned, as [ARCHITECTURE.md](ARCHITECTURE.md) explains.
- It is not a legal authority, a court, or a replacement for a professional.
- It does not send a visitor's text to a service unless the visitor has chosen that service, and the app says so.

## Who it is for

People who want to explore a question from several angles, and developers who want to help build a careful, testable conversation tool.
